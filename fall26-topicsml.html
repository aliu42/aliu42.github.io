Course Description

This class aims to showcase various ways that theoretical insights play a role in advancing modern machine learning. Topics include scaling laws, data and compute allocation, post-training and reinforcement learning, and inference-time interventions. We will emphasize theoretical perspectives that both help us understand modern training pipelines and inform new algorithms and design principles.
